⚡ Home Energy Coach • ऊर्जा बचत सहायक

Home Energy Coach is a citizen-friendly, bilingual (English & हिंदी) web dashboard designed to help households understand their monthly electricity bills in 10 seconds, identify power-hungry appliances without technical jargon, and build simple daily habits to cut electricity bills by 10% every month.

🔗 Live Website: https://tanishq206.github.io/home-energy-coach/

✨ Key Features

🏠 10-Second Monthly Overview: View monthly electricity spend (₹8/unit), kWh consumption, weather impact, energy score (/100), and a personalized 10% savings target across an 8-month dataset.

⏰ Time-of-Day Appliance Clock: Clear visual guidance on Cheaper Hours (10 PM – 6 AM), Normal Hours (10 AM – 4 PM), and Costly Peak Hours (4 PM – 10 PM) plus an interactive daily family saving checklist.

📊 Interactive Analytics & Graphs: Dynamic Chart.js visualizations comparing monthly electricity trends, 24-hour home load spikes, and appliance-wise bill distribution.

🛡️ Playful "Easy View" Power Meter: Zero-jargon 10-block visual meters showing how much power each appliance consumes, with interactive "Try Saving Tip" buttons to simulate instant savings.

🛜 Smart Home Auto-Savers (IoT Control): Interactive automation toggles (Smart AC Peak Saver, Night Wash Scheduler, Geyser 20-Min Auto-Off, Empty Room Auto-Off, and Vacation Mode) with real-time annual savings calculation.

🎙️ Voice-Enabled AI Energy Coach: Ask questions via voice or quick prompts in English or Hindi, complete with spoken audio responses.

📱 Built-in Desktop & Mobile Preview Switcher: Toggle between full-screen desktop layout and a mobile device frame directly from the top bar.

🛠️ Tech Stack

Frontend: HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)

Styling: Tailwind CSS (CDN), Google Fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans & Noto Sans Devanagari)

Data Visualization: Chart.js

Voice & Audio: Web Speech API (SpeechRecognition & SpeechSynthesis)

Hosting: GitHub Pages (Zero-build, single-file architecture)
